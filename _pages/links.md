## Open Source Neuromorphic Software

[Fugu](https://github.com/sandialabs/Fugu)
Fugu is an intermediate representation for describing neuromorphic algorithms in a composable graphical manner.  [Link to paper.](https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3354265.3354268)

[scANNs](https://github.com/sandialabs/scANNs)
scANNs are a strategy for Monte Carlo probabilistic sampling of artificial neural networks (ANNs).  [Link to paper.](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/10386770/)

[Whetstone](https://github.com/sandialabs/Whetstone)
Whetstone is a method for converting standard ANNs directly to spiking neural networks (SNNs).  [Link to paper.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-018-0015-y)

[Neurons 2 Algoriths (N2A)](https://github.com/sandialabs/n2a)
N2A is a model description language for describing and composing systems of dynamical elements, such as large scale neural models.  [Link to paper.
](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neural-circuits/articles/10.3389/fncir.2014.00001/full)

[Neuromorphic Finite Elements (NeuroFEM)](https://github.com/sandialabs/NeuroFEM)
NeuroFEM is a technique for modeling finite elements on neuromorphic hardware.  [Link to paper. ](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-025-01143-2)

[Neuromorphic Random Walks (NeuralRW)](https://github.com/sandialabs/Neural-Random-Walker)
NeuralRW is a technique for modeling discrete time Markov Chain random walks on neuromorphic hardware.  [Link to paper.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41928-021-00705-7)  

[Graphical Neural Activity Threads (GNATs)](https://github.com/sandialabs/GNATFinder)
GNATs are a technique for decomposing spatio-temporal graphical causal relationships from neuroscience spiking data.  [Link to paper.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.16684)

[STACS](https://github.com/sandialabs/STACS)
STACS is a tool for natively multi-core large scale neural simulations.  [Link to paper.](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/2634-4386/ad3be7/meta)
